Suleyman Kiroglu

about

I am a PhD student in Sociology at the University of Colorado Boulder, where I am also pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science. I am a research assistant at the University of Colorado Population Center, and I hold an M.A. in Near Eastern Studies from New York University.

My research is in social demography and population health. I work on mortality and life expectancy in the United States and on migrant organizations and local politics in Turkey.

suleyman.kiroglu@colorado.edu · google scholar

research

Organizations, place, and political life. Hometown associations among Istanbul’s internal migrants, and the social bases of local political elites.

Population health, migration, and mortality. U.S. mortality relative to peer countries, migration and state life expectancy, and economic connectedness and premature adult mortality.
