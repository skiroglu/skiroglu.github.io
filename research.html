research

My research is in social demography and population health, across the three areas below.

mortality and population health

As a research assistant at the University of Colorado Population Center (CUPC), I work on a project on early-life mortality trends in the United States, 1980–2024.

Three related manuscripts are in preparation:

  • Early-life Mortality and the Widening U.S.-Peer Longevity Gap
  • The Effects of Immigration and Domestic Migration on U.S. State Life Expectancy Trends
  • Economic Connectedness and Premature Adult Mortality Across U.S. Areas

migration and migrant organizations

I study hometown associations in Istanbul, organizations formed by internal migrants who share a place of origin. This work has examined how the associations are classified and organized, how they are distributed across the city, their organizational density, and the organizational cohesion of internal migrants.

  • Mapping Solidarity: Organizational Density of Hometown Associations in Istanbul (article, 2024)
  • Hometown Associations and Organizational Cohesion of Internal Migrants in Istanbul (presentation, 2024)
  • Classification, Organization, Distribution and Density of Hometown Associations in Istanbul (presentation, 2022)

local politics and brokerage

This work examines the social bases of local political power in Turkey, with attention to rural notables, professionals, and ideologues. It also asks who acts as a broker, and examines representation, brokerage, and social capital in local politics.

  • Rural Notables, Professionals, and Ideologues: The Social Bases of AKP Local Power (article, 2026)
  • Who Brokers? Representation, Brokerage and Social Capital in Local Politics (presentation, 2025)
  • Who Brokers? Representation, Brokerage and Social Capital in the Turkish City (presentation, 2025)

methods and training

My work draws on demographic data, quantitative and computational methods, and sociological theory. Selected coursework includes natural language processing, machine learning and spatial data, big data architecture, and data visualization. Tools I use include Python, Stata, and ArcGIS. Alongside the PhD in Sociology, I am pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at CU Boulder.
