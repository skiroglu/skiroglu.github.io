research

My research is in social demography and population health. One body of work examines mortality and life expectancy in the United States. Another studies migrant organizations and local politics in Turkey.

mortality and population health

As a research assistant at the University of Colorado Population Center (CUPC), I work on a project on early-life mortality trends in the United States, 1980–2024.

Three manuscripts in preparation are also in this area. The first studies early-life mortality and the widening longevity gap between the United States and its peers. The second examines the effects of immigration and domestic migration on life expectancy trends across U.S. states. The third examines economic connectedness and premature adult mortality across U.S. areas.

  • Early-life Mortality and the Widening U.S.-Peer Longevity Gap (in preparation)
  • The Effects of Immigration and Domestic Migration on U.S. State Life Expectancy Trends (in preparation)
  • Economic Connectedness and Premature Adult Mortality Across U.S. Areas (in preparation)

migration and migrant organizations

I study hometown associations in Istanbul, organizations formed by internal migrants who share a place of origin. This work has examined how the associations are classified and organized, how they are distributed across the city, their organizational density, and the organizational cohesion of internal migrants.

  • Mapping Solidarity: Organizational Density of Hometown Associations in Istanbul, Turkish Studies (2024)
  • Hometown Associations and Organizational Cohesion of Internal Migrants in Istanbul, Population Association of America Annual Meeting (2024)
  • Classification, Organization, Distribution and Density of Hometown Associations in Istanbul, Migration Studies Graduate Student Conference (2022)

local politics and brokerage

Another line of work concerns local politics in Turkey. A published article examines the social bases of local political power, with attention to rural notables, professionals, and ideologues. Related presentations ask who acts as a broker, and examine representation, brokerage, and social capital in local politics and in the Turkish city.

  • Rural Notables, Professionals, and Ideologues: The Social Bases of AKP Local Power, European Politics and Society (2026)
  • Who Brokers? Representation, Brokerage and Social Capital in Local Politics, American Sociological Association Annual Meeting (2025)
  • Who Brokers? Representation, Brokerage and Social Capital in the Turkish City, Foundation for Sciences and Arts (2025)

methods and training

My work draws on demographic data, quantitative and computational methods, and sociological theory. Selected coursework includes natural language processing, machine learning and spatial data, big data architecture, and data visualization. Tools I use include Python, SQL, Stata, and ArcGIS. Alongside the PhD in Sociology, I am pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at CU Boulder.
