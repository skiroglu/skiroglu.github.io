research

My research focuses on migration, political organization, and population health. I study how social ties and population change are distributed across places, and how those patterns relate to political representation and mortality.

Organizations, place, and political life

Mapping Solidarity

Published · Turkish Studies · 2024

This article studies differences in hometown-association organization among Istanbul’s internal migrant groups. It measures the number of associations relative to each origin group’s population and compares patterns by province of origin and across Istanbul.

Method
DERBIS association records + TURKSTAT migration data · 81 origin provinces
Finding
Organizational density varies sharply across migrant groups and is concentrated in a contiguous region between the Black Sea hinterland and Eastern Anatolia.

Rural Notables, Professionals, and Ideologues

Published · European Politics and Society · 2026

This article examines how local political authority is organized through AKP district municipal councils in Istanbul. Using biographical data and civil-society affiliations for 684 council members, it identifies recurring social bases of local political elites.

Method
684 municipal council members · prosopography · K-modes clustering
Finding
District councils bring together rural-notable, professional, and ideological pathways into local office, reflecting distinct social bases of political authority.

Population health, migration, and mortality

Early-life Mortality and the Widening U.S.–Peer Longevity Gap

Manuscript in preparation

This manuscript examines how mortality before age 50 contributed to the widening U.S.–peer life-expectancy gap from 1980 to 2023, and which ages and causes drove the change.

Method
U.S. mortality records + Human Mortality Database + WHO · 19 peer countries · age- and cause-specific decomposition
Finding
Early-life mortality worsened relative to peer countries across every stage from infancy through young adulthood.

Migration and State Life Expectancy

The Effects of Immigration and Domestic Migration on U.S. State Life Expectancy Trends

Manuscript in preparation

This manuscript examines how immigration and domestic migration changed state population composition between 1990 and 2019, and how those changes were related to male and female life-expectancy trends.

Method
50 states · six age groups · 1990–2019 · Census/ACS + U.S. Mortality Database · decomposition and fixed-effects models
Finding
Migration substantially reshaped state populations and was strongly associated with changes in state life expectancy.

Economic Connectedness and Premature Adult Mortality

Manuscript in preparation

This manuscript examines whether Economic Connectedness, the extent of cross-class friendship ties, is associated with premature mortality among adults ages 25–44 across 972 U.S. areas from 2017 to 2024.

Method
972 analytic areas · ages 25–44 · 2017–2024 · mortality records + Social Capital Atlas
Finding
Higher Economic Connectedness is associated with lower premature mortality across U.S. areas, suggesting that cross-class social connectedness may be one dimension of place-based health inequality.
